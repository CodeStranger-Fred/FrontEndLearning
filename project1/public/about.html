About Deer

Despite their enchanting presence, Westlake deer face growing threats due to habitat loss and human encroachment, requiring urgent conservation efforts to safeguard these graceful creatures and their delicate ecosystems.

Our Services

Habitat Preservation

Collaborating with private landowners, we incentivize habitat preservation on private properties. Research and monitoring guide our efforts, ensuring the health and vitality of deer habitats. By creating wildlife corridors and no-hunting zones, we connect fragmented landscapes and provide safe spaces for these graceful creatures.

Hunting Management

We liaise with wildlife agencies to ensure that hunting regulations align with conservation goals. By actively engaging hunters, we promote ethical and sustainable hunting practices that help control deer populations while preserving the long-term health of their ecosystems. We strike harmonious balance between deer management and conservation.

Emergency Response

Our specialists assess and manage disease outbreaks, limiting their impact on deer populations. We assist in rescue and rehabilitation, ensuring that injured or orphaned deer are treated and released back into their natural habitats. Saving Deer in Danger stands ready to respond to crises and protect these magnificent creatures when they need it most.
